Skip for now
Assessment Specs
Articulate Storyline 360 • 8 questions from a pool of 15 • Pass mark: 75% • 2 attempts allowed • Results via SCORM 1.2 to LMS • Feedback on all incorrect answers with regulatory reference • Personalised result slide based on score variable
Assessment Design Philosophy
Every question in this assessment sits at Bloom's Level 3 or 4 (application and analysis), not simple recall. The distractors, the wrong answers, are based on real misconceptions gathered during the needs analysis. A learner who picks the wrong option learns something useful from the feedback, not just that they were wrong.
Full Specification
  • 8 questions drawn from a randomised pool of 15
  • 4 question types: multiple choice, drag-and-drop matching, true or false with justification, and short labelling
  • Immediate feedback per question with cited regulation on incorrect attempts
  • Personalised result slide: "Your weakest area was Place of Supply Rules" using a score variable
  • SCORM 1.2 compliant, pass or fail and score reported to LMS
  • Tax SME reviewed all items for regulatory accuracy before publish
How I evaluate after launch
LMS analytics track item difficulty, discrimination index, and most-missed questions. I review these every quarter and update the question pool when patterns show a consistent misunderstanding. On the most recent revision cycle, average second-attempt scores rose to 88%.
